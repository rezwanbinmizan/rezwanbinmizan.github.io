# Photo Gallery Images

Add your photos to this folder with these recommended names:

## Profile Photos
- `profile-photo.jpg` - Your professional headshot (used in hero section)

## Conference Photos
- `conference-1.jpg` - Mathematical Opportunities in Digital Twins Conference
- `conference-2.jpg` - SIAM Texas-Louisiana Section Conference  
- `conference-3.jpg` - TAMIDS Scientific Machine Learning Summer School

## Poster Photos
- `poster-1.jpg` - Kuramoto-Sivashinsky Equation ROM poster
- `poster-2.jpg` - POD-ROMs for gKSE poster

## Research Photos
- `research-1.jpg` - Research visualization or lab work
- `research-2.jpg` - Additional research activities

## Tips:
- Use JPG or PNG format
- Recommended size: 1200x800 pixels or larger
- Keep file sizes under 2MB for faster loading
- Use descriptive filenames

Once you add photos with these names, they will automatically appear in your portfolio gallery!
